House rules
& tips

A few things that help everyone enjoy their stay.

House rules

  • Quiet hours from 22:00 to 08:00. Neighbours live here all year round.
  • No smoking inside the apartment. Please use the balcony.
  • No parties or events. Only registered guests may stay overnight.
  • Pets are not allowed unless agreed in advance.
  • Please keep the keys safe and close all windows and the door when you leave.
  • Report any damage or breakage to the host as soon as you notice it.

Pool & community areas

  • Shower before entering the pool. No glass in the pool area.
  • Follow the opening hours and rules posted by the residential complex.
  • Do not leave towels or belongings on the pool loungers overnight.
  • Children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

Tips for using the apartment

  • Air conditioning: switch it off when you open the windows or go out.
  • Water and electricity: please use them sensibly, especially in summer.
  • Rubbish: sort it and take it to the containers outside the building, not to the balcony.
  • Toilet: flush only paper. Wipes and hygiene products block the pipes.
  • Leave the kitchen tidy and the dishes washed before check-out.

Check-out

  • Turn off the lights, air conditioning and appliances.
  • Put used towels in the bathroom and bring the rubbish out.
  • Lock the door and leave the keys as agreed with the host.
Thank you for taking care of the apartment and the neighbourhood.
